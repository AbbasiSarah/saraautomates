Solutions

Four workflows that take routine work off reception.

Start with one, or set up all four together. Each is built around your clinic’s services, timings and rules.

Booking · Reminders · Enquiries · Handover

Online booking

Patients choose a service and a free time, and the appointment goes straight into your calendar.

  • Shows only genuinely free slots
  • Allows for treatment length and dentist
  • Instant confirmation to the patient

Reminders

Automatic reminders before each appointment, with simple confirm and reschedule options.

  • 24-hour and 2-hour reminders
  • Reschedules release the old slot
  • Confirmed status visible to reception

Patient enquiries

Everyday questions answered from your approved list: hours, location, prices, parking, what to bring.

  • Answers only from your approved FAQs
  • Your clinic’s tone of voice
  • English, or English and Arabic

Staff handover

When a question needs a person, it goes to reception with the full conversation so nobody has to ask twice.

  • Insurance, complaints and anything medical
  • Urgent messages flagged first
  • Daily summary each morning

Common questions

Do patients know they’re talking to an assistant?

Yes. It introduces itself as the clinic’s assistant and offers a person whenever needed.

Can it give medical advice?

No. Anything medical is passed to your team, and urgent messages are flagged first.

Do we need a new phone number?

Usually not. We use your existing clinic number on the official WhatsApp Business platform, or your current SMS provider.

What if we want to stop?

Monthly care is month-to-month. Your calendar and data stay yours.
