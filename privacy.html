Security & Privacy

How your information and your patients’ information are handled

Last updated: November 2026

This website

This site is a static website served over HTTPS. It has no logins, no database and no advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

The public demo

The demos use fictional clinics, fictional appointments and preset choices. They never ask for your name, phone number or medical details. Nothing you click in a demo leaves your browser or reaches a real clinic calendar.

The “Book a demo” form

When you send the form, your business contact details and the information you choose to share are sent over HTTPS to a secure Google service, checked again on the server, and stored in a private Google Sheet that only Sara can access. Repeated or automated submissions are blocked. Your details are used only to reply to your request and are never sold or shared. Ask at any time and they will be deleted.

Please don’t include any patient information in the form.

Live clinic systems

  • The public demo is completely separate from any live clinic system.
  • Each clinic gets its own credentials with only the permissions it needs.
  • Before a booking is saved, the server checks the slot is still free, which prevents double bookings.
  • Automation tools are kept private behind two-factor authentication. API keys stay on the server and are never placed in web pages.
  • Sensitive data in automation logs is removed or kept to a minimum.
  • The assistant answers only from the clinic’s approved questions and hands everything else to staff. It never gives medical advice.
